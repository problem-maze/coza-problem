## Meaning of the composition

Nature itself forms the eye:

- upper natural wood/branch mass: upper lid-like boundary,
- lower natural wood/rock/vegetation mass: lower lid-like boundary,
- lake: iris-like field,
- central island/tree mass: pupil-like center,
- sunlight reflection: catchlight,
- distant mountain/forest depth: environmental context rather than graphic decoration.

The scene must read as nature first and an eye second. Do not draw a literal eye on top.
