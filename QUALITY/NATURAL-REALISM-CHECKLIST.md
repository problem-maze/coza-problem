# Natural Realism Checklist

A Natural Eye build is not visually accepted if any of these are obvious:
- short repeating water cycle,
- identical wave motion across the entire lake,
- shoreline or island edge sliding,
- reflection moving independently of the water,
- regular sparkle spacing,
- waterfall that looks like moving dashes,
- global fog sheet,
- synchronized foliage,
- trunks or rocks moving,
- whole-image breathing or wobble,
- motion amplitude that exists only in diagnostics but disappears at phone scale.

Positive signals:
- stable boundaries,
- irregular local variation,
- multiple spatial/temporal scales,
- material-specific motion,
- depth-dependent amplitude,
- long non-obvious recurrence,
- visually coherent energy direction,
- quiet regions that remain quiet.
