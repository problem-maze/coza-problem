# Acceptance Gates

## Z0
Phone screenshot; exact image visible; correct 3:2 composition; no asset failure.

## Z1
Phone screenshot with debug geometry. Manual inspection confirms moving masks do not include static wood, rocks, or island mass.

## Z2
One owner, monotonic clock, user pause/resume, background/foreground recovery, no unexpected clock reset.

## Z3
Short phone video. Water movement is unmistakable. Island and shoreline are visibly stable. Counters alone do not pass this gate.

## Z4
Phone video. No obvious short cycle, no rubber-image appearance, no moving shore seam.

## Z5
Reflection rides the water and does not expose an independent rhythm.

## Z6
Waterfall reads as continuous downward motion with separate impact/spray; no marching dash pattern.

## Z7
Atmosphere improves depth without looking like a moving overlay sheet.

## Z8
Near foliage moves; trunks/rocks do not; clusters are not synchronized.

## Z9
The user perceives one living environment rather than separate effects.

## Z10
60-second benchmark and lifecycle tests pass the agreed envelope or produce a documented, user-approved tradeoff.

## Z11
Home integration passes Natural/Moon theme switching, PageLife ownership, CTA/Solve navigation, I18N, Bottom Nav, reduced motion, and tier-low fallback.

## Z12
No unresolved critical regression; final phone evidence and benchmark are stored; build is version-locked.
